About Horizon Institute

Founded in 1998, Horizon Institute has grown from a regional technical college into a premier center for academic excellence and innovation.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver accessible, practical, and transformative education that equips learners to solve real-world challenges.

🔭 Our Vision

To be a globally recognized hub for technological research, entrepreneurship, and ethical leadership.

🏛️ Accreditation

Fully chartered and accredited by the Commission for Higher Education and ISO 9001 certified.
